I don’t like Chrome’s new 2023 UI and want to keep the original look. Please turn off the 2023 refresh by setting both of these feature flags to Disabled and relaunching Chrome: chrome-refresh-2023 (Chrome Refresh 2023) and chrome-webui-refresh-2023 (Chrome WebUI Refresh 2023). You can refer to this article: https://bugartisan.medium.com/disable-the-new-chrome-ui-round-in-2023-db168271f71e
