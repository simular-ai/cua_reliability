My glasses are broken, and I’m having trouble seeing small things clearly. On my Ubuntu (GNOME) desktop, could you make the text larger system-wide (not just in one app)? Using the Accessibility “Large Text” option (so the text scaling is about 1.2x or higher) is perfect; alternatively, turning on the built-in Screen Magnifier with the zoom set above 1.1x also works. Here’s the reference I’m looking at: https://help.ubuntu.com/lts/ubuntu-help/a11y-font-size.html.en
