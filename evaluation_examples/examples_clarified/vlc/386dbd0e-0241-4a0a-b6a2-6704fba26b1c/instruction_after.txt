I’m reading a lecture PDF while VLC plays in the background, and I don’t want to switch apps just to pause or resume. Could you configure VLC so that play/pause works via a global keyboard shortcut even when VLC isn’t the active window, without minimizing the PDF reader? Please set VLC’s Global Play/Pause hotkey (not the regular one) to a sensible key like the media Play/Pause key or Ctrl+Alt+Space, and save it so it persists across restarts (i.e., written to VLC’s vlcrc at ~/.config/vlc/vlcrc, updating the global-key-play-pause entry). Here’s the related post for context: https://superuser.com/questions/1708415/pause-and-play-vlc-in-background?rq=1
