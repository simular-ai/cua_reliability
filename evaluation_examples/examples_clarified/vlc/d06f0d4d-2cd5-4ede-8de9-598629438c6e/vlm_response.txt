Could you make the volume slider dark by setting VLC’s slider color configuration to a black-ish palette? Please update the qt-slider-colours entry in VLC’s vlcrc (usually at ~/.config/vlc/vlcrc) to a semicolon‑separated list of RGB values (0–255) for the slider gradient, with every R, G, and B component below 100 so it stays dark in low light. If qt-slider-colours isn’t present, add it so the change persists after relaunch. This is for the Qt interface slider used by the volume control. Here’s the context I was looking at: https://superuser.com/questions/1039392/changing-colour-of-vlc-volume-slider</answer>
