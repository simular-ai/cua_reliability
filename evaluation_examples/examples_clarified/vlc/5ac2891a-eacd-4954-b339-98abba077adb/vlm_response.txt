On this Linux desktop, please stop VLC from auto-quit behavior by making sure the “Quit at end of playlist” option in the Media menu is turned off. If that toggle isn’t available in this build, set the equivalent preference (Tools > Preferences > Playlist > “When finished”) to “Do nothing.” I’d like this to persist so VLC doesn’t close itself after a video or playlist ends in future sessions.
