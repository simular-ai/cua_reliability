Create a folder named “Promotions” under Local Folders (not under the IMAP account), then set up an enabled incoming‑mail filter for the Outlook account (outlook.office365.com) that moves messages from its Inbox when the Subject contains the lowercase word “discount” to Local Folders > Promotions (use the “Move to folder” action). Leave the Message Filters window open when you’re done. Reference: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/organize-your-messages-using-filters
